Pros

Cons

  • More radical design change
  • May feel restrictive

  • See total set count at a glance
  • Spatial consistency maintained

Cons

  • More visual clutter with many sets
  • Takes more horizontal space
  • Disabled buttons might confuse
  • Not as clean/minimal
📋 Note: Both proposals prevent users from accidentally skipping sets while showing progress clearly. Proposal 1 is cleaner and more minimal, while Proposal 2 maintains spatial consistency and lets users see all sets at once. Choose based on your preference for minimalism vs. information density.
