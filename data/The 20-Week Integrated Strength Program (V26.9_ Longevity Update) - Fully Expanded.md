# **The 20-Week Integrated Strength Program (V26.9: Longevity Update) \- Fully Expanded**

## **Global Protocols (Reference)**

**The "Cold Start" Rower Protocol (Perform before Day 1 & 5\)**

* Minute 1–2: Easy Rower (Zone 1\)  
* Minute 3–4: Moderate Rower (Zone 2\)  
* Minute 5: 3 x 10s Sprints (Wake up CNS)

**Global Tendon Prep (Perform immediately after Rower)**

* 20 Band Pull-Aparts  
* 15 Band External Rotations (per side)  
* 3 x 5 Scapular Pull-ups with 3s ISO-HOLD at top (No bouncy reps)  
* 20–30s Passive Bar Hang

**Ankle Mobility Circuit (Perform on Day 2\)**

* Knee-to-Wall Mobilization: 2 mins/side  
* Tibialis Wall Raises: 3 sets of 15–20 reps  
* Deep Squat Iso-Hold: 2 sets of 30s

# **BLOCK 1: FOUNDATION (Weeks 1–4)**

**Block Objectives:** Establish volume baseline (36–40 reps), reinforce squat patterns, build tissue tolerance.

## **WEEK 1**

**Summary:** Establishing the volume baseline with moderate intensity.

### **Day 1: Pull Volume (Workout A)**

**Daily Prep**

* **Rower Protocol:** Minute 1–2 Easy, Minute 3–4 Moderate, Minute 5 (3 x 10s Sprints).  
* **Tendon Prep:**  
  * 20 Band Pull-Aparts  
  * 15 Band External Rotations (per side)  
  * 3 x 5 Scapular Pull-ups (3s Hold)  
  * 20–30s Passive Bar Hang  
* **Skill Practice (Crow Stand):** 3 sets of 10–20 seconds.

**The Workout**

* **A1: Pull-Up Ladders:** 4 sets of (1, 2, 3 reps) – Total 24 reps.  
* **B1: Ring Rows:** 3 sets of 15 reps (Tempo 2-1-1-0).  
* **B2: Incline Push-Ups:** 3 sets of 10 reps (Tempo 2-1-1-0).  
* **C1: Hollow Rocks:** 3 sets of 25 seconds.

**Cool Down (Volume Reset)**

* **Passive Dead Hang:** 30–60s (Spinal decompression).  
* **Butcher’s Block Stretch:** 60s (Targeting Lats/Triceps).  
* **Cobra Stretch:** 60s (Releasing abs).  
* **Wrist Extensor Stretch:** 30s per side.

### **Day 2: Recovery \+ Anti-Rotation \+ Ankles**

**Mobility & Core**

* **Ankle Mobility Circuit:**  
  * Knee-to-Wall Mobilization: 2 mins/side.  
  * Tibialis Wall Raises: 3 sets of 15–20 reps.  
  * Deep Squat Iso-Hold: 2 sets of 30s.  
* **Shoulder Balance:** Band Internal Rotations (2 sets of 20/side) OR Light Supinated Pulldown (2 sets of 15).  
* **Core/Flow:** Mobility Flow \+ Pallof Press (3 sets of 12 reps).

**Cool Down (System Reset)**

* **Child’s Pose:** 2 minutes (Focus on breathing into lower back).  
* **Box Breathing:** 3 minutes (4s In, 4s Hold, 4s Out, 4s Hold).

### **Day 3: Posterior Chain \+ Knee Health**

**The Workout**

* **A1: Romanian Deadlift (RDL):** 4 sets of 8 reps @ \~85kg.  
* **B1: Weighted Glute Bridge:** 3 sets of 10–12 reps.  
* **B2: Goblet Squats:** 3 sets of 10 reps (Light/Technical focus).  
* **C1: Face Pulls:** 3 sets of 15–20 reps.

**Cool Down (Post-Hinge)**

* **Couch Stretch:** 90s per side (Critical for hip flexors).  
* **Light Weighted Jefferson Curl:** 2 minutes (Spinal segmentation).  
* **90/90 Hip Rotations:** 10 reps per side.

### **Day 5: Pull Intensity \+ Push (Workout B)**

**Daily Prep**

* **Rower Protocol:** Minute 1–2 Easy, Minute 3–4 Moderate, Minute 5 (3 x 10s Sprints).  
* **Tendon Prep:**  
  * 20 Band Pull-Aparts  
  * 15 Band External Rotations (per side)  
  * 3 x 5 Scapular Pull-ups (3s Hold)

**Part 1: Primary Lifts**

* **A1: Weighted Pull-Ups:** 3 sets of 5 reps @ 8–10kg. (Use Double Progression).  
* **B1: Dips:** 3 sets of 10 reps (Bodyweight).

**Part 2: Accessories (10-Minute Hard Cap)**

* **Minute 2:** Bulgarian Split Squat (Right Leg) – 8 reps.
