## Athlete Profile & Goals

| | |

| **Bench Press** | 90 kg |

**Primary Goals:** Improve overall strength, fitness, and calisthenics compression skills (L-sit, Pike) without the need for 1RM peaking. Add unilateral stability.

**Injury Considerations:** Tennis Elbow & Goose Foot.

**Schedule:** Lifting on Mon, Wed, Fri. Active recovery on Tue, Thu.

**Post-V26.9 Transition Note:** If this block starts immediately after the 21-week Integrated Strength Program, treat Weeks 1-2 as barbell reintroduction, not performance weeks. That prior plan exits through taper, test, and reload work with relatively little straight-bar Bench or Back Squat exposure. Use the listed loads as ceilings and bias toward crisp technique over load chasing.

## Injury Management & Coaching Rules

### The Elbow
Use a hook grip for all Cleans. Use neutral grips for all pressing and pulling accessories. The straight-bar Bench Press is your **only** non-neutral grip press. Ring pull-ups are preferred over fixed-bar — rings allow natural wrist rotation mid-rep, reducing elbow stress.

### The Knee
Maintain tripod foot pressure and let the knees track over the toes during all squats. Box Squats will remain strictly a lower-intensity accessory.

**Unilateral Rule:** For step-ups and single-leg work, prioritize a slow descent (eccentric) and perfect knee tracking to build stability without flaring the goose foot.

### Cardio Defaults
Assault/Echo Bike and SkiErg. Only row if absolutely symptom-free (use a loose "hook" grip).

### Pain Scale & Readiness Rule
- Pain must stay at or below **3/10**
- Symptoms must return to baseline within **24 hours**
- If they do not: reduce volume by **20–30%** next session
- **Optional accessories:** Only perform if elbow pain during bench was 2/10 or less AND feels 100% normal the next day

### Autoregulation Rule
- Treat the listed percentage as the **ceiling**, not a forced target
- **Weeks 1-4:** Keep main lifts around **RPE 6-7**
- **Weeks 5-8:** Keep main lifts around **RPE 7-8**
- **Week 10:** Cap all work at **RPE 8**
- If bar speed drops clearly, positions break down, or pain rises: reduce load by **2.5-5%** or cut **1 set**

### Post-Program Handoff Rule
- **Bench:** If straight-bar work feels rusty after the prior program, use **2.5-5 kg less** than listed in Weeks 1-2, then resume the written progression once bar path and elbow tolerance feel normal
- **Power Clean:** If catches feel unfamiliar after the prior block's high-pull and taper work, start Week 1 closer to **60%** and rebuild to the listed loads by Week 2
- **Back Squat / Box Squat:** If Week 21 reload left you underexposed to barbell squatting, reduce Week 1 by **5 kg** and only move to the listed Week 2 loads if recovery is normal within 48 hours
- **Ring Pull-Ups:** Keep them **bodyweight through Week 6** unless elbows are fully calm and all reps stay crisp

### Hinge Rule (RDLs)
Keep shins vertical. Stop at deepest pain-free hamstring position. RDL weights are scaled at roughly **40–55% of your 155 kg Deadlift max**. If medial knee irritation or hamstring tightness lingers into Saturday, reduce next week to 2 sets or shorten ROM.

### Olympic Lifting Rule
If catches get sloppy or slow, convert remaining sets to Clean Pulls to preserve the joints.

## Mandatory Warm-Up (Before Every MWF Session)

**General:** 3–5 min light Bike or SkiErg.

**Knee/Glute/Stability Prep:**
- Single-Leg Glute Bridges — 2×10/leg, pause at top
- Unweighted Single-Leg RDLs / Airplanes — 2×8/leg for hip stability
- Lateral Band Walks — 2×15 steps/side

**Elbow/Wrist Prep:**
- Wrist Rolls — 15 reps each direction
- Eccentric Wrist Extensions — 2×15/arm with 1–2 kg dumbbell

**Core:** Hollow Body Hold — 2×20 seconds

**Lift-Specific Ramp-Up:**
- Take 3-5 progressive warm-up sets before the first main lift
- For Bench: add 1-2 light sets of scapular retraction/depression work
- For Cleans: add front rack mobility and 2-3 crisp turnover/high-pull drills
- On Mondays for the first 4 weeks after V26.9, add **2 light sets of straight-arm band pulldowns or scapular pull-ups** before Ring Pull-Ups

---

## Post-Session Cooldown (After Every MWF Session)

- 3-5 min easy Bike or SkiErg cool-down spin
- **Elbow/Wrist:** 2×15 eccentric wrist extensions + 30-second forearm extensor stretch/arm
- **Lower Body:** 30-second/side each: hip flexor stretch, pigeon stretch, standing quad stretch
- **Shoulder/Chest:** 30-second/side doorway pec stretch + band pull-aparts 15 reps

---

## Weekend Recovery (Saturday & Sunday)

- **Saturday:** Full rest or 20-30 min easy walk. Foam roll any sore areas. No loaded training.
- **Sunday:** Optional 20-30 min easy Bike/SkiErg + 10-15 min full-body stretching. Use this as a body-check day — note any lingering elbow or knee symptoms before Monday.
- If Saturday pain is above baseline, apply the **Pain Scale & Readiness Rule** to Monday's session.

## PHASE 1: Accumulation & Tendon Prep (Weeks 1–4)

**Focus:** 3-second eccentric on Bench. 3-second pause at the bottom of the Back Squat. Building tissue capacity and rebuilding straight-bar familiarity after the prior pull-focused block.

---

### WEEK 1

#### Monday — Bench Focus & Compression

**A. Bench Press** — 4×8 @ 65% (57.5 kg) — *3-second negative*

**B. Ring Pull-Ups** — 3×5–8 *(Bodyweight — use a slow 2-sec negative; rings allow natural wrist rotation to protect the elbow)*
> **Progression rule:** Start conservative. Add reps before adding weight. If elbow flares, switch to ring rows for that session.

**C. Parallette L-Sit Practice** — 4×8–12 seconds (Tucked hold)
> **Progression rule:** Build from 8s toward 12s across Phase 1. Advance to one leg out only when all 4 sets are held with locked elbows, depressed shoulders, no lumbar collapse, and no shaking.

**D. Accessory** *(2 Rounds)* — 10–12 DB Rows/arm (Neutral) + 10–12 Single-Arm DB Neutral Floor Press/arm
> OPTIONAL: Skip if elbow is above 2/10 pain. Start with 2 rounds in Weeks 1-2 to let the tendons adapt before adding a 3rd round in Weeks 3-4.

**E. Conditioning** — 4 Rounds: 30s Heavy Sled Push (or heavy bike sprint) / 90s rest

#### Tuesday — Active Recovery
- **Cardio:** 20–30 min easy pace (Bike/SkiErg)
- **Mobility:** 15 min adductor/hamstring stretches + 3×15 eccentric wrist extensions

## PHASE 2: Intensification (Weeks 5–8)

**Note:** If knee or elbow symptoms trend upward in Weeks 6-8, drop either the clean pulls or the unilateral accessory first before reducing the main lifts.
